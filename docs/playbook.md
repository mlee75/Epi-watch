# Clipping playbook

## Accounts
- One dedicated account per niche (e.g. a GTA clips account), on TikTok, Instagram and YouTube.
- Clipping Nation warm-up (required for their campaigns):
  - Day 1 and 2: scroll 30+ min in the niche, watch to the end, like 15 to 20, write 5 to 7 real comments, follow 8 to 10 niche accounts. No posting.
  - Day 3: one test video from the phone app. After 24h: 1,000+ views = good, under 200 = start a fresh account.
  - First 10 posts: upload from the phone app only. No bio link until views are steady.
- Connect each account in contentrewards.com > Settings > Accounts and in Whop > Settings > External accounts.
- Likes must stay visible. Keep every clip live 30 days.

## Audience
- Many campaigns require English and a share of Tier 1 viewers (US, UK, CA, AU). Posting from France pushes local reach first.
- Prefer campaigns with no Tier 1 rule until the account has a Tier 1 audience.
- Some campaigns need a screenshot of the country breakdown once a clip passes the minimum views. Send it in their Discord.

## Daily loop
1. `/run` in Claude Code (or `/clip <campaign>` for one stream).
2. `/review`: keep the best, reject the rest. One great clip beats ten rushed ones.
3. Post from the phone, copy the caption from the app, paste the post link in the app.
4. Submit the link in Content Rewards within 30 minutes.
5. `/stats` once a day.

## Before joining a new campaign
- Net rate after the 10% fee, views needed for min and max payout, budget left, clippers already in.
- Read the full rules page, not only the card. Follow the stricter one when they disagree.
- Red flags: upfront fees, paid "communities", crypto payment, pool campaigns with very high view minimums.
