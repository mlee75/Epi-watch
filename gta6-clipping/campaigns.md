## Fee and payout rules (Content Rewards, current version)
- CPM campaigns: 10% creator fee taken from each payout when it settles. Source: contentrewards.com/articles/clipping-payouts-explained
- A clip earns for 30 days after you submit it. New payout round about every 10 days, short hold, then withdraw from $1 to Whop balance (free), then to bank/PayPal (standard up to 5 business days).
- Pool campaigns (Reach): paid when the pool ends. Fee on pools not stated on the pages checked.
- Every campaign: brief details, reference files and extra requirements stay hidden until you join.

## Content rules: what the pages say
- No page checked mentions spoilers or cutscenes. Reach's rule pages say nothing on it either. Clip free-roam, chaos and streamer reactions, not story missions, to stay clear of Rockstar takedowns.
- Reach (Lacy, Clix, SypherPK): streamer must be the main focus, name + hashtag in caption, no logos, no AI, no reposts or collab posts, 50%+ audience in native-English countries, submit within 30 min of posting, one strike = permanent ban from all Reach campaigns.
- Seal: 10s minimum, "Kick.com/SealStats" visible on self-edited clips.
- Ruckus: GTA 6-specific account required.
- No campaign checked asks for money upfront except GTA6 Clip Network.

## Reality check
- Total GTA-specific CPM money still open: about $4,900 (TOMO $990 + Bobby $973 + Ruckus $1,000 + Tokyo Girl $1,912), shared by every clipper who joins this week.
- Each $1K budget equals 500K to 1M paid views total. These drain within days once launch traffic hits.
- A new account with zero followers in France rarely clears 10K views per clip in week one. Expect tens of dollars, not hundreds, unless a clip breaks out.
- TikTok and Reels push new accounts to local audiences first. A French audience fails the Reach 50% English rule.
