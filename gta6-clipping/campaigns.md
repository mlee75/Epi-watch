## What to check on each campaign in Whop
1. Budget remaining vs. total, and how many clippers have joined
2. Rate after the platform fee (~10% reported)
3. Per-clip cap: how many views until a clip stops earning
4. Allowed platforms (YouTube Shorts is often excluded)
5. Footage source: provided by the campaign, or you capture it yourself?
6. Review/approval rules and payout hold period
7. Who actually owns the campaign: the streamer/brand, or a middleman

## Reference benchmarks
- Median game campaign: ~$1.50 / 1k views (49 campaigns); all campaigns ~$1.00 (721 campaigns)
- Median TikTok gaming campaign budget: ~$1,350
- Whop's minimum budget for pay-per-view (CPM) campaigns: $1,000
