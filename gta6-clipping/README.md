# GTA 6 Clipping — Monetization Project

Started 2026-11-18 (launch eve). GTA 6 unlocks at local midnight on 2026-11-19, rolling from New Zealand.

> Note: this folder lives in the Epi-Watch repo only because that is the repo this session has.
> It is unrelated to Epi-Watch — move it to its own repo when convenient.

## Goal
Make money from GTA 6 launch-week attention, and come out of it with an audience and
streamer relationships that outlive launch week.

## Monetization paths (fastest first)
| # | Path | Time to first $ | Typical rate | Main risk |
|---|------|-----------------|--------------|-----------|
| 1 | Paid clipping campaigns (Content Rewards / Whop) | Days (10-day hold reported) | ~$0.70–$2 / 1k views, per-clip caps $35–$300 | Few funded GTA 6 campaigns; budgets run out |
| 2 | Own accounts → platform payouts | Weeks–months | TikTok ~$0.40–1.00 / 1k (60s+ videos, 10k followers) | Thresholds; YouTube downranks unoriginal re-uploads; Rockstar "non-commercial" grey zone |
| 3 | Sell managed clipping to mid-size streamers | 1–2 weeks | Retainer / % of campaign budget | Unproven — our hypothesis |

## Content rules (non-negotiable)
- No story cutscenes, ending, or big plot reveals (Rockstar copyright policy, Jan 2025).
- Only clip streamers who have a campaign or gave permission.
- Add value: captions, hook in first second, translation (ES → EN is the best angle found).
- Post natively per platform — no cross-platform watermarks.

## Kill criteria
After 7 days: if earnings/hour < ~$10 and not trending up → change angle or stop.

## Files
- `campaigns.md` — campaigns found, with verification status
- `tracker.csv` — log every clip posted: views, payout, hours spent
